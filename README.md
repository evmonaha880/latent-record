# The Latent Record

The companion record for *Forgetting the Man Who Manufactured Memory*: every saved state of one essay (587 states, 2026-08-03 → 2026-08-21), laid out as seven contact sheets, every paragraph openable in place with a slider through every distinct text it had, and under each stop the cause of that text: the margin note it answered, the message that set the save going, the directive, the round.

Live: https://evmonaha880.github.io/latent-record/

By Evan Monahan. Built by `build_site.py` from `edits.json`, the essay's single edit record; every figure on the page carries its method and the record's verifier passes 56 checks before a build. Layers on the page: states and transitions · 272 margin notes (46 fragments and 2 artifacts not shown) · 22 machine saves against the hand saves · the hand-typed floor (983 of 5,530 traced words) · 10 rounds · 96 directive rows · 15 outside artifacts with provenance · the hand layer: every note and every essay-era message ask read and placed by hand, reviewed row by row by the author on 2026-09-08 · cut material shown as it stood · the *Pale Fire* cap asserted on the largest single view.
