# The Latent Record

The companion record for *Forgetting the Man Who Manufactured Memory*, an essay about George Eastman and the company he built. The essay is the front page. Every paragraph opens in place, with a slider through each save it went through (568 saves, 3 to 21 August 2026), the author's margin notes where he wrote them, and, on the published text, each sentence's own history.

Live: https://evmonaha880.github.io/latent-record/

By Evan Monahan, written with Claude in Claude Code. Every figure on the site is one of the edit record's counts and carries its method; the record's verifier passes 76 of 76 checks before a build.

Around the essay: `exchanges/` (the paragraphs drafted in the chat window) · `before/` (the evening of 3 August: the plan, the outline and the thesis candidates) · `cut/` (every sentence ever written, by what became of it) · `how/` (the models and dates, what the record proves and does not, how to cite a draft, the Wayback captures).

Cite a paragraph at a save: `#p29@537` (also `#p29@pass9`, `#p29@2026-08-20T14:01:42`). Save numbers are permanent; citations to the earlier `essay/` address and to the 09/08 page forward to the same save.
