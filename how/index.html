How it was made

The Latent Record is one essay, Forgetting the Man Who Manufactured Memory, about George Eastman and the company he built, shown with every save of it. It is an essay about Eastman, not about its author or his tools. The record is here so that a reader can see what changed at each save, and what the author wrote in the margin before it changed, without taking anyone’s word for it.

Written by Evan Monahan with Claude in Claude Code, 3 August to 21 August 2026. Two models wrote: Claude Opus 5 (claude-opus-5) from 3 August, 22:02 to 19 August, 13:51, 618 turns; Claude Fable 5 (claude-fable-5) from 19 August, 13:57 to 21 August, 11:13, 501 turns. The model changed once, on 19 August. A reviewing subagent ran once on Claude Sonnet 5 (claude-sonnet-5), 4 August, 17:13; it read the draft and wrote nothing into it.

Two things the page does not show

Which words the author typed himself is not shown: the editor saved every few seconds whether he was typing or pasting, and the record cannot yet tell the two apart. A filled dot on the slider names a save made after a Claude write; it names the save, not who decided the words.

The record

Every figure on this site is one of the record’s counts and carries its method. The record is one file, built 2026-09-14 16:20 Pacific, this build 2026-09-23 17:32. It holds 568 saves and 567 transitions between them, 248 margin notes, 66 paragraphs, 520 sentences ever written of which 255 are in the published text. Its digest: 03370e5c890ac96c02b78256e5a143664c09cdb790e15ee70115d4ec80aea017.

What the verifier proves

76 of 76 checks passed on the record this site was built from. They recompute the record from the raw files rather than trusting it: every saved state rehashes from its body and reconstructs from its raw file; every save a layer cites exists; the notes, rounds, sentence chains and hand files agree with one another and with the live essay; the last save precedes the lock and no later session wrote the file.

What it does not prove

  • Before the essay · The record begins on the evening of 3 August 2026. The fifteen months of research before it, much of it in other chat tools, survive only in fragments.
  • Research outside Claude Code · Two research inputs inside the three weeks came from another tool and are not in the record: a reading of the MIT plaque from a photograph on 4 August, later found wrong, and a long research run on 19 August.
  • Days with no saves · There are no saves from 5 to 16 August or on 18 August. The essay was not worked on in those days.
  • Reading and thinking · What the apparatus read and what the author handed it are recorded with times. What he read on paper or off screen, and what he thought, are not.
  • Copies sent to readers · Copies shared with early readers as documents sit outside the record, and any comments on them are not in it.
  • Pruned backups · Some backups from 4 August were pruned before the record was built; one save there survives as its text only.
  • Who made each save · A save is marked as coming after a Claude write when one came just before it. Every other save is the editor saving while the author worked. The log is not complete, so a few of those may be Claude’s.
  • What kind of note · Whether a margin note is an instruction, draft prose or a fragment is read by hand, not measured.

Citing a draft

A paragraph at a save is cited by the paragraph’s number and the save’s permanent number: https://evmonaha880.github.io/latent-record/#p29@537. #p29@pass9 opens the last save of the ninth pass, #p29@2026-08-20T14:01:42 the last save at or before that time, and +notes opens the all-stages list. A save number is assigned once and never reassigned; when the record folds a save that only opened an empty note into the save before it, the old number stays a valid citation. 19 such aliases stand; the map is record/state_map.json. Citations made to the earlier address of the essay page, essay/#p29@537, forward to the root.
